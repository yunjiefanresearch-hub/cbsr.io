About the register

The Cross-Border Stablecoin Register (CBSR) is an independent research project, maintained by Yunjie Fan since January 2026. It records how twelve jurisdictions regulate stablecoins and how their rules combine along cross-border corridors. Each record keeps its source, date and review status in separate fields.

The code was written with AI coding assistants. Yunjie Fan sources the legal records and records each one's review state. The data use CC BY 4.0 and the code uses Apache-2.0.

who made it

Yunjie Fan is an independent researcher. The working papers are posted on SSRN and have yet to undergo peer review. Policy submissions include written input listed by the US SEC Crypto Task Force (May 2026), a comment on US Treasury GENIUS Act rulemaking (September 2026), and a response to the European Commission’s targeted MiCA review consultation (September 2026). An article appeared on the FinReg Blog (Duke Financial Economics Center) in June 2026.

using the research

The register is for research. Seek qualified legal advice for specific decisions. In v0.11.0, zero records are decision-ready. No record has a current source check or an independent second review. Check the cited source and the date it describes before relying on a record.

what this site collects

The site uses no analytics or tracking scripts.

Your language preference is stored in your browser. Maintainer applications are sent through a form relay to the project’s email inbox; you can also contact the maintainer directly by email.
