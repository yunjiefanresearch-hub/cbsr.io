about  ·  independent research, open infrastructure

Independent research. Shared infrastructure.

CBSR builds evidence infrastructure for agentic finance. Its first published dataset, the Cross-Border Stablecoin Register, brings cross-border stablecoin regulation into a source-linked, versioned research system. Explore the dataset guide for coverage, releases and documentation.

The project connects legal research, structured data and open-source tools so researchers and builders can work from the same underlying record.

who made it

Yunjie Fan is an independent researcher on cross-jurisdictional digital-asset regulation. The research includes SSRN working papers, written input posted by the SEC Crypto Task Force, and an article on Duke Law's FinReg Blog. The register, build pipeline, evidence model, and interactive map are maintained by the author as open data and open code. A public posting does not imply institutional endorsement or adoption.

Research independence

The register takes no compensation, direct or contingent, from any issuer, exchange, or intermediary in it, and there is no advisory, consulting or employment relationship tied to it.

Research conclusions follow the published sources and methodology. Any future funding or relevant professional relationship will be disclosed on this page.

Development focuses on depth across twelve jurisdictions: primary-source research, corridor analysis and reproducible releases. The public review worklist gives contributors a practical way to strengthen that foundation.

using the research

Each release is a dated research snapshot, identified by its version and source commit. For professional use, check the record’s primary source, legal status, date and review status. Available archive identifiers are listed in the repository.

Record-level sourcing and review information supports independent assessment. The dataset is research material; legal advice and execution authorization remain with the relevant professionals and institutions.

what this site collects

The site uses no analytics or tracking scripts.

Your language preference is stored in your browser. Maintainer applications are sent through a form relay to the project’s email inbox; you can also contact the maintainer directly by email.
