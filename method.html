methodology  ·  from primary law to structured research

A clear path from source to system.

CBSR connects primary legal sources, structured propositions, corridor analysis and typed tools. Its architecture preserves the distinctions researchers need to assess a record: claim type, source quality, legal effect, date and review status.

5 architectural layers  ·  6 build layers  ·  3 evidence axes  ·  schema validation and reconciliation checks

The research architecture

Each layer carries a defined responsibility, from preserving primary-source provenance to retaining the dataset version in downstream workflows.

L1

Evidence layer

Primary instruments, official gazettes, supervisory rules and commencement orders. This layer preserves the source, pinpoint and reading date.

L2

Knowledge layer

Structured propositions covering jurisdictions, instruments, dimensions, obligations, entities and relations. Claim type distinguishes legal propositions from market observations.

L3

Reasoning layer

Constraints, interaction sets, compatibility and temporal logic combine into jurisdictional analysis. Derived results retain the supporting rules and evaluation date.

L4

Agent layer

Typed MCP tools provide schema-validated responses and access to the citable subset. Coverage and review status accompany the records used by an agent.

L5

Action layer

Institutions apply the research in routing, settlement, treasury, filing or supervision workflows. Those systems retain responsibility for execution and should preserve the dataset version in their records.

how these five relate to the six build layers below

The architecture describes responsibilities; the six build layers below describe implementation. L1–L2 map to build layers 01–02, L3 to 03–06, and L4 to the MCP server. Institutions provide L5.

Three independent evidence axes

Every record identifies its claim type, source tier and legal status. Their intersection defines the structural-candidate set. Additional freshness and independent-review requirements govern inclusion in the strict citable subset; inspect those fields at record level.

claim_class = tier1_legal ∧ status = in_force ∧ evidence_tier = resolution_text  →  46 structural candidates
Axis The question it answers Distinction preserved
claim_class Is this a proposition of law, or an observation about what the market does? Market practice and binding legal requirements remain separate.
evidence_tier Does this rest on official text, or on a paraphrase, a news report, or a supervisor's remark? Regime-level confirmation and provision-level verification remain distinct.
status Is this instrument in force today? Enactment and commencement are recorded separately.

why three axes rather than one score

Separate fields preserve the different questions each axis answers. Source quality, claim type and legal effect are evaluated on their own terms; the build applies explicit inclusion rules to the citable subset.

the implementation  ·  six build layers, each derived from the one before it

The six build layers, and what each one recomputes

Each build layer derives from the preceding data. Recomputed results are compared with authored analysis, with discrepancies surfaced for review.

01

Obligation records

The base facts. One sourced (jurisdiction × instrument × dimension) proposition of law per record, schema-validated.

02

Analysis layer

The pairwise compatibility matrix and the six constraint-interaction sets: the composition analysis, turned from prose into queryable data.

03

Corridor layer

Directed origin → destination edges, each read at the inbound gate, carrying its feasibility class and the mechanism that gates it.

04

Computed layer

compose() derives corridor classes from the per-jurisdiction signal table and compares them with authored analysis. Differences are reported for review.

05

Time engine

compose(as_of=…) applies the dataset’s scheduled legal changes at a selected date. The recorded UK commencement, for example, changes the modeled US → UK classification on 25 Oct 2027.

06

Constraint substrate

Corridor classes are composed from each jurisdiction’s stance on eight constraints (C1–C8), using the constraint-interaction rules.

Validation and reconciliation

Automated checks cover sourcing, claim classification, commencement, reconciliation and schema validity. Negative test cases verify that invalid records are rejected.

Gate What it refuses The failure it prevents
Sourcing A record cannot claim a confirmation it has no source for. A confirmation level quietly promoted.
Claim class A market fact cannot enter the instrument a lawyer is shown. Industry practice served as legal requirement.
Commencement A statute that is written but not commenced cannot be cited as current law. A pending regime read as an operative one.
Reconciliation A recomputed corridor class that contradicts the authored analysis cannot merge silently. Two places in the dataset disagreeing, both looking right.
Schema A record that does not validate cannot enter the build. Every downstream consumer guessing at field shapes.
