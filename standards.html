Open regulatory data. A wider field of use.

the tradition it sits in

Connected research traditions

The register draws on established approaches to machine-readable law, regulatory technology and source-based analysis.

Line of work What it does Where the register stands
Rules as code Producing an authoritative machine-readable version of a rule alongside the rule itself, maintained by the body that owns it. A third-party research interpretation with primary-source links, precise provisions and versioned records.
RegTech Making it cheaper for a regulated firm to discharge its obligations: reporting, screening, monitoring. Source-linked analysis of the legal conditions underlying regulatory workflows and cross-border market access.
SupTech Giving supervisors a data-shaped view of the market and of peer regimes. Comparable public-source research across jurisdictions, dimensions and directed corridors.
Agent grounding Giving an AI system that acts a record it can cite rather than paraphrase. Typed MCP tools connect structured regulatory records to agent research and application workflows.

research scope

The register identifies applicable mechanisms, supporting provisions and legal status at a specified date. Transaction-level compliance requires the relevant facts, professional assessment and institutional controls.

Development-policy connections

The mapping connects four SDG targets with relevant dataset mechanisms and records. These are subject-matter connections, with scope stated for each target. CBSR has no UN affiliation or endorsement.

SDG 10.ccorridor research

Reduce remittance transaction costs, and eliminate corridors costing more than 5 per cent

Target. By 2030, reduce migrant remittance transaction costs to below 3 per cent and eliminate remittance corridors costing more than 5 per cent.

Mechanism in the register. Directed corridors identify the regulatory conditions relevant to cross-border access, including equivalence requirements and separate authorization paths. These records provide legal context for research on remittance corridors.

Backing records. 132 directed edges, 6 feasibility classes, 16 scheduled reclassifications. claim_class = tier1_legal.

Scope. The register provides regulatory context for corridor research. Remittance prices and measured cost outcomes require separate data.

SDG 17.18open data

Increase the availability of high-quality, timely and accessible data

Target. Increase the availability of high-quality, timely and reliable data, disaggregated along relevant dimensions.

Mechanism in the register. Open licences, a public schema and versioned releases support reuse. Records can be compared by jurisdiction, regulatory dimension and directed corridor, with dates and review information retained.

Backing records. The dataset’s sourced records, public schema and record-level review fields.

Scope. An independent research dataset for comparative analysis, separate from official statistics and SDG indicator reporting.

SDG 16.4AML obligations

Significantly reduce illicit financial flows

Target. By 2030, significantly reduce illicit financial and arms flows, strengthen recovery and return of stolen assets, and combat organized crime.

Mechanism in the register. AML and customer-due-diligence records, including FATF Travel Rule material, support comparison of legal obligations across jurisdictions.

Backing records. The per-jurisdiction records under the aml_kyc dimension and the Travel Rule entries.

Scope. Legal obligations concerning AML and due diligence. Transaction monitoring and estimates of illicit flows require separate evidence.

SDG 8.10access conditions

Expand access to financial services

Target. Strengthen the capacity of domestic financial institutions to encourage and expand access to banking, insurance and financial services for all.

Mechanism in the register. Investor-gating and retail-access records distinguish professional-investor requirements, transaction caps and other legal conditions affecting market access.

Backing records. The per-jurisdiction records under the investor-gating and retail-access dimensions.

Scope. Regulatory conditions affecting eligibility and market access. The mapping makes no measured claim about changes in financial inclusion.

the mapping is data too

A structured policy mapping

The proposed structure below records the target, mechanism, supporting material and scope in a consistent form for comparison and review.

// analysis/sdg_mapping.json — one row per mapped target
{
  "sdg_target":              "10.c",
  "target_text":             "…",
  "mechanism_in_register":   "corridor_layer",
  "backing_records":         ["corridors/*", "transitions/*"],
  "claim_class":             "subject_matter_alignment",
  "what_this_does_not_claim": "no measurement of remittance cost",
  "as_of":                   "2026-08-20"
}

claim_class = subject_matter_alignment identifies these rows as policy mapping, separate from legal propositions returned by citable_law().
