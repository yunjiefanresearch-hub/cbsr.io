How the register relates to rules as code and RegTech

The register provides a third-party research interpretation. Consult the issuing authority for the official text. Firms remain responsible for their own compliance assessments.

the tradition it sits in

Connected research traditions

The register draws on established approaches to machine-readable law, regulatory technology and source-based analysis.

Line of work What it does Where the register stands
Rules as code Producing an authoritative machine-readable version of a rule alongside the rule itself, maintained by the body that owns it. A third-party research interpretation with primary-source links, precise provisions and versioned records.
RegTech Making it cheaper for a regulated firm to discharge its obligations: reporting, screening, monitoring. Source-linked analysis of the legal conditions underlying regulatory workflows and cross-border market access.
SupTech Giving supervisors a data-shaped view of the market and of peer regimes. Comparable public-source research across jurisdictions, dimensions and directed corridors.
Agent grounding Providing records that AI systems can cite directly. Typed MCP tools return records with their source, date and review status.

research scope

The register identifies applicable mechanisms, supporting provisions and legal status at a specified date. Transaction-level compliance requires the relevant facts, professional assessment and institutional controls.
