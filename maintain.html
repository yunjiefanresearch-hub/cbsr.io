what the role is

The jurisdiction maintainer role

Contribute your knowledge of a legal system through focused, part-time research. Each contribution connects a regulatory proposition to the official text, its exact provision and its applicable date.

What a maintainer does
  • 01Verify records against official legal text and attach exact article or section references.
  • 02Track pending instruments and update recorded commencement dates when official information changes.
  • 03Submit source-based corrections where the record and primary text diverge.
  • 04Review corridor classifications, mechanisms and direction, including destination-side requirements.
Contribution principles
  • 01Contribute research material. The role is independent of a legal advisory engagement and creates no client relationship.
  • 02Contribute in a personal capacity, with individual attribution and relevant interests disclosed.
  • 03Use publicly available sources that another researcher can inspect and verify.
  • 04Agree a practical research scope and prioritize precise, well-supported records.

    application review

    Show us how you read the record.

    Include one concrete example: a record you would correct or strengthen, together with the primary provision supporting your reading.

    The example helps establish your research approach and identify a useful first contribution. Independent researchers and graduate students are welcome.

    Applications are sent to the maintainer’s inbox and usually receive a reply within two weeks. If you have not heard back, please follow up directly by email.

    Apply to maintain a jurisdiction

    Name, email, jurisdiction and the research example are required. Additional background and availability help us agree a practical scope of work.

    1 · Who you are

    Context only. Independent researchers and graduate students are as welcome as anyone.

    2 · What you would maintain

    Jurisdictionrequired

    More than one is allowed, though one is usually more convincing than four.

    Indicate the time you can contribute each month, including occasional or project-based availability.

    3 · The specific cell

    This section matters more than the rest of the form. An application without it still submits; one with it is read first.

    Any format. For example: “JP → HK is recorded as Category II, but under Article Y of Act X that determination is not a precondition to entry, so it should read Category I.” If you think nothing is wrong, name a cell where you could raise a regime-level confirmation to a provision-level one instead.

    Official gazette, a statutory database, supervisory circulars, or something else. This decides how deep a cell can be checked.

    4 · Background and published work

    Papers, regulatory submissions, blog posts, GitHub repositories all count. Volume is not the point; anything that shows how you read a provision is. Blank is fine.

    Disclose relevant commercial, advisory or employment relationships involving you or your employer and an issuer, exchange or intermediary covered by the register.
