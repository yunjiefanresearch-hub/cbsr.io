Independent research · Open data

An open register of stablecoin rules in twelve jurisdictions.

The Cross-Border Stablecoin Register records what each jurisdiction requires of stablecoin issuers and intermediaries, the sources available for those records and the dates they describe. It also analyses 132 directed corridors, assessing entry and exit rules separately.

What the register holds

Three ways into the same records: by jurisdiction, by corridor, or from software.

Jurisdictions

Issuer rules, reserves, holder yield and market access in each jurisdiction, with source and review fields for each record.

Directed corridors

Method

Each record keeps the legal proposition, source, scope and date in separate fields, together with the strength of the source and the review it has had. To qualify as citable current law, a record must pass all checks in the methodology. In v0.11.0, zero records qualify.
